# WeChat Moments Short Copy

> Each post runs about two lines on a single Moments screen and is published with an image — do not let it read like a template. Every item is "one concrete sentence", not a slogan.
> How to use: replace the 【】 placeholders, pick one item for the occasion, and choose the image from your current materials (real photos of the clinic > stock images).

2. 攒了大半年，【商家名】开门了。没什么大场面，就是把诊室、理疗区、消毒流程一样样收拾到位，等第一个客人上门。地址在【地址】，欢迎来坐坐。
3. 第一位客人刚走，送了句"挺干净"。这评价收下了。【商家名】在【地址】，【营业时间】。

## Usage notes

- At most one or two posts a day; three ads in a row will get you blocked — space them out with "day-at-the-clinic" items like No. 6.
- The numbers in the copy (prices, quotas, times) must match the live campaign; when the campaign changes, remember to come back and update the post.
- Never invent cases. Only de-identified cases (consent obtained + personal details removed) may be used that way; once a fabricated story is exposed, nothing you posted before will be believed either.
- None of the terms "自研" (self-developed), "行业领先" (industry-leading), "对标××" (benchmarking ××), "全网最低" (lowest on the internet) may appear in any post — the same banned list as in [使用说明](./index#pre-publish-checklist).
