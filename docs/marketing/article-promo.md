# Template 3: Promotional Campaign

> When to use: limited-time campaigns for festivals / clinic anniversaries / member days. Publish one article on the official account, and post the short copy from [moments](./moments) to WeChat Moments in parallel.
> How to use: pin the rules down and spell them out — time, offer, quota, how to participate. Never publish a campaign with vague rules; your front desk will be flooded with questions.

---

**Title options**

- 【商家名】冬季调理专场：立冬开始，预约从今天起
- 【活动名】开始报名：名额、时间、规则都在这一篇
- 【商家名】周年庆：三件事，没有第四件

**Body copy**

【商家名】的【活动名】开始了。规则不复杂，一次讲完。

**活动内容**

1. 【活动内容一】，e.g. 首次体验价 XX 元（原价 XX 元）；
2. 【活动内容二】，e.g. 到店消费满 XX 元送 XX；
3. 【活动内容三】，e.g. 老客带新客，双方各享 XX。

(Three items are enough. The more offers a campaign has, the harder people have to calculate — and the fewer end up joining.)

**时间与名额**

- 活动期：即日起至【活动截止】；
- 每日限号【名额】个，约满为止（工作日通常比周末好约）；
- 到店出示【参与凭证】参与，e.g. 本条推文 / 朋友圈截屏 / 小程序券码。

**怎么参加**

【预约方式】提前约，【电话】可咨询。馆所在【地址】，【营业时间】。

**三条说明**

- 活动价与其他优惠不叠加，到店时以店内公示为准；
- 涉及具体项目的服务由医生面诊后决定是否适用，不适用会当场说明，不会先做了再收费；
- 有疑问先打【电话】问清楚再来，别白跑一趟。

【商家名】【地址】【电话】

---

**Rewriting tips**

- Prices and quotas must be real and enforceable. If you write "10 per day", you must truly cap it at 10 — accept the loss when someone no-shows; only then will "limited to 10" be believed the next time.
- "Limited time" must come with a concrete end date: fill in the 【活动截止】 date, never "end of the month" or "as soon as possible".
- Do not use countdown-style lines like "last day" or "lowest ever" in the copy — say those only when the deadline has actually arrived.
- During the campaign, post progress updates to WeChat Moments: "3 slots left today", "booking opens at 10 tomorrow". Real progress works better than re-sharing the full article.
